Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

At PlayGrid Arcade, protecting visitor privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy describes what information is stored locally on your device, how third-party advertising partners such as Google AdSense may operate, and the choices available to you.

1. Browser Local Storage (Scores & Preferences)

PlayGrid Arcade does not require user registration or login. High scores, favorite game bookmarks, sound mute settings, light/dark theme preferences, and cookie banner choices are stored exclusively inside your browser’s localStorage and never leave your device to any PlayGrid database.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

When advertising is enabled on this website, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet:

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to PlayGrid Arcade and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, visitors can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Log Files & Hosting Infrastructure

Like most static websites hosted on GitHub Pages or content delivery networks, standard server access logs may temporarily record non-personally-identifying technical metadata (browser type, referring page, date/time stamp, and IP address) strictly for security and delivery reliability.

4. Children’s Privacy

PlayGrid Arcade does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. Because our games require no accounts, forms, or chat rooms, children can play without submitting personal details.

5. GDPR, CCPA & Consent Choices

Visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and California may manage cookie consent directly from our consent banner or clear browser storage anytime through browser settings. To request assistance or ask privacy questions, please reach us via our Contact Page.
